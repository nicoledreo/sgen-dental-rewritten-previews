Why it keeps coming back

Painkillers treat the ache. They do not treat the reason.

The pain moves

Lower back one month, neck and headaches the next. Pain that travels usually shares one mechanical cause, and treating each site separately never resolves it.

The exercises are generic

A sheet of stretches is built for an average body, not for how yours actually loads, moves and compensates. Useful sometimes. Rarely sufficient.

Nobody examined you properly

The most common thing we hear is that no one had run a full orthopaedic and neurological exam before. That is the part that got skipped, and it is where we start.

Back and spinal care

Persistent lower-back pain, sciatica and disc-related symptoms. We assess for cause — load, mobility, and how the pelvis and hips are behaving — before anything is adjusted, and we tell you honestly if imaging or a GP referral is the right next step instead.

Neck and headaches

Tension headaches and neck stiffness that track back to the upper spine and the way a working day is spent at a screen. Treatment is paired with changes to the setup that caused it, because otherwise you are back in six weeks.

Posture and scoliosis

Structural assessment for postural collapse and spinal curvature. We measure, we show you what we measured, and we are clear about what chiropractic care can change and what it cannot — which for structural curvature is an important distinction.

Athletic performance

For runners, lifters and court athletes: range restored, load tolerance rebuilt, and the injury pattern interrupted rather than managed around. We will work with your coach if you have one, and we re-test rather than assume.

Before you book

Do I need a referral from my GP?
No. Chiropractic is a primary-contact profession, so you can book directly. If we think your symptoms need a GP or a specialist rather than us, we will tell you at the assessment and write to them with your consent.
Will I be adjusted on the first visit?
Only if the assessment supports it and you are comfortable going ahead. The first appointment is ninety minutes precisely so the examination and the explanation are not squeezed. Some people leave with a plan and no treatment at all.
How many visits will I need?
We give you an estimate in writing after the assessment, because it depends on what we find. Most patients tell us they feel a difference inside three visits. We review the plan as we go, and we end care when you no longer need it.
Does it hurt?
Adjustments are not usually painful, though short-lived soreness afterwards is common, much like after exercise. We tell you what to expect before anything happens, and nothing proceeds without your agreement.
Can I claim on private health insurance?
Many policies cover chiropractic care, and cover varies by insurer and plan. We issue itemised receipts for every appointment. Check with your provider before booking so you know where you stand.
