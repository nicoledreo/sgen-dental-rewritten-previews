About the practice

We assess before we treat.

Fulcrum is three clinics and twelve registered chiropractors, built around one rule that is easier to state than to run a business on: nobody gets adjusted before they get explained to.

Book your first visit See what we treat

Most people arrive here having already tried the obvious things. The pain quietened, then came back in the same place, because nothing changed about what was causing it. So we spend the first ninety minutes finding out what that is — and if the answer is that we are not the right people to fix it, we say so and tell you who is.

Dr Maya Aldridge

Clinical lead, all three clinics · registered chiropractor · 14 years in practice

Maya took over the practice in 2019 and rebuilt the first visit from scratch. The old protocol ran thirty minutes and ended with an adjustment. The new one runs ninety and ends with a written plan — sometimes without an adjustment at all, if the assessment does not support one.

She holds an MSc in Sports Rehabilitation and still runs a full caseload two days a week, mostly the referrals other practitioners have not been able to place. If your pain has already outlasted two or three attempts at fixing it, hers is usually the desk it lands on.
