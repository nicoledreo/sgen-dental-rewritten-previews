02

Why you can feel fine at the roadside and not on Thursday

When your head is thrown suddenly in any direction, the muscles, ligaments and connective tissue of the neck and upper back take the load.

At the roadside you are full of adrenaline. The stiffness builds over the following days. That lag is ordinary, and it is why people are surprised by Thursday.

Getting examined early gives us the clearest picture of the injury.

  • Week oneNeck stiffness. Headache. Shoulder and upper back ache.
  • AlsoLow back pain. Poor sleep. Difficulty concentrating.

What the first visit after a collision involves

  • A conversation about the crash and what you are feeling

    Before anything is examined.

  • A hands-on examination

    The practice examines before deciding anything.

  • Digital x-rays, if the examination indicates them

    Not everyone needs one.

  • A plain explanation of what was found, before anything is done

Some symptoms need a medical doctor before they need us

  • Severe or worsening headache
  • Changes to your vision
  • Confusion
  • Weakness in an arm or a leg
  • Chest pain or breathlessness after a crash

Any of those means urgent medical assessment first.
