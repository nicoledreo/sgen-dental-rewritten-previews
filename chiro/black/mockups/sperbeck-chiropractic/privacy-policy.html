What we collect

This website ships no form and no online submission of any kind. What reaches us is therefore limited to: what you tell us by telephone, what you choose to write in an email to info@tracychiropractor.com, and the analytics described below.

Separately from the website, the new patient intake form collects health history. That form is completed at the office or downloaded and brought in, not submitted online.

Why we collect it

To arrange your visit and to provide and document your care.

Patient health information

This is a healthcare practice, so some of what we hold is protected health information. It is held by the practice and seen by the doctor and the office staff who need it to do their work.

Email is ordinary email and is not a secure channel. Please do not put detailed health information in a message to us. If you need to tell us something sensitive, call the office or tell us at your visit.

You may ask to see your records.

Cookies and analytics

This site loads a Google Tag Manager container. Any analytics it carries are described here once the build's final tag set is fixed.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold, ask for a correction, and ask us to stop contacting you.

Changes to this policy

A full privacy policy, including the notice a California chiropractic practice owes its patients, is being prepared with the practice's counsel and will replace this text.
