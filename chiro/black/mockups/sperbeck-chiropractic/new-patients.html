Your first visit

What actually happens, step by step, from the moment you sit down.

Patient forms

Complete the new-patient paperwork at home. Then the appointment starts with your questions instead of a clipboard.

What actually happens on visit one

Step one

  • You describe what hurts and what happened to cause it.

Step two

  • A detailed history then an orthopaedic and neurological examination.

Step three

  • Diagnostic x-rays only if they are needed.

Step four

  • Dr. Sperbeck explains what he found and what he proposes, before anything is adjusted.
