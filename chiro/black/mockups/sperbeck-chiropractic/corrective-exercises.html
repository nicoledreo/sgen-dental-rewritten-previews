Corrective Exercises in Tracy, CA

Simple movements taught in the room and reviewed at your next visit, so the adjustment has a better chance of holding.

One of the six services this practice provides.

Book your first visit

Why exercises are part of the care here

Tight, overworked muscles pull a joint back toward the position that hurt in the first place.

That is why an adjustment on its own can stop holding between visits. That joint gets moved, then the muscle quietly moves it back.

The exercises are how you interrupt that at home, between appointments.

Who teaches you the movement

Dr. Sperbeck does, in the room, on the visit the exercise is given.

He watches you perform the movement, and he corrects it there and then. You do not leave with a sheet of diagrams and a guess.

More about him on Dr. Sperbeck.

What you are actually given

Two or three movements. Shown once, corrected on the spot, and written down so you have them at home.

Most of them take a few minutes.

That is genuinely all it is. There is no programme to buy and no tier above it.

Doing them at home, adapted to you

What a seventy-year-old is given is not what a warehouse worker is given.

Dr. Sperbeck is gentle enough to work with older patients, children, and pregnant patients, and the movements follow the same principle.

If a movement sharpens the pain, stop, and say so at the next visit. That is useful information, not a failure.

And where it belongs with someone else: he will work with your medical doctor, or refer you to an appropriate specialist, if your condition requires it.

What this is not

  • Not a gym programme
  • Not supervised physical therapy
  • Not a replacement for anything your medical doctor has prescribed

This practice does not provide physical therapy, and this page will not imply that it does.

The same doctor, every visit

The movement you were given in March is reviewed by the same person in May, in the same room.

One named doctor, one location, thirty years in Tracy.

That is what makes an exercise plan progress rather than restart. The same principle applies to a low back and to a neck.

Where it starts

Corrective exercises are not sold separately, and no separate fee for them is published.

They begin after the examination, which is what the entry offer covers: the $39 new patient special includes a complete comprehensive consultation and examination, plus x-rays if needed. Its terms are on that page.

What the appointment itself involves is set out on the visit walkthrough, and the questions page covers the rest.

Where this goes next

What you are booking is an examination

Not a class, not a course, and not a plan sold by the block. The movements come after someone has looked at you, and they are given in the room on the day.

If none is appropriate for you, none is given, and you will be told that rather than handed a sheet.
