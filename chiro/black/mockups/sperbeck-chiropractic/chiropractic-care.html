In plain language

A chiropractic adjustment is a controlled, measured movement applied to a spinal joint.

Its purpose is to improve how that joint moves, and to ease the pain that comes with it not moving well.

That is the whole of the claim on this page.

Who this is for

Who this page is written for

Some of you were adjusted forcefully somewhere else and never went back.

Some of you have never been adjusted at all and are dreading the sound.

And some of you are here because of a car accident or something that happened at work, and the pain has outlasted your patience with it.

All three are the right reader for this page.
