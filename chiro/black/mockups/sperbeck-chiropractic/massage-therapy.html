Massage Therapy in Tracy, CA

Therapeutic massage inside a chiropractic office, used to ease the muscle tension around the area being treated.

Call (209) 836-5558

What this is, and what it is not

This is therapeutic massage aimed at the soft tissue around a musculoskeletal complaint.

It is not a relaxation service and it is not a spa booking. It is also not a substitute for the adjustment.

If what you want is an hour of quiet and some music, this is the wrong room, and we would rather you knew that before you arrived than after.

Who delivers it

Massage therapy is available at this practice, as one of the six services it provides.

We are not going to describe a team we have not introduced you to. When the name and credential of the therapist are published, they will be on this page.

How it pairs with a gentle adjustment

Working the surrounding muscle first means less force is needed at that joint.

For a reader who is nervous about being adjusted, that is the whole reason this service is on the list.

That is the intent behind pairing them, and what patients tell us.

It is not a guarantee, and this page puts no time period on how long an adjustment holds.

More on chiropractic care.

What a session involves

  • ClothedYou stay clothed in whatever you can move comfortably in.
  • TargetedThe work is on the muscle around the complaint. Neck, shoulders, low back, whatever the examination pointed at.
  • ExplainedYou are told what is being worked on before it is worked on.
  • Yours to stopYou can say stop at any point.

That last one is not a courtesy line. It is how the session runs.
