Non-Surgical Spinal Decompression in Tracy, CA

A drug-free, non-surgical option used for some disc-related low back pain, delivered by one chiropractor who has practised in Tracy for thirty years.

Call (209) 836-5558

How non-surgical decompression actually works

You lie on a motorised table. A harness holds your pelvis.

  • PullThe table applies a slow, measured pull along the length of your spine.
  • HoldThe cycle eases the compressive load across the disc for a short interval.
  • ReleaseThen it releases it.
  • RestAnd that pattern repeats through the session.

It is not sudden and it is not forceful. The table moves slowly and you can feel every part of it coming.

The table itself

Alongside the decompression table, the practice's equipment includes flexion traction for herniated discs, hydraulic lift tables, and computerised digital x-ray.

The make and model of the decompression table is not stated on this page yet. It is not recorded in anything this rebuild has been given, and this office would rather leave the line blank than print a brand name it has not verified.

Call and ask, and the front desk will tell you exactly what is in the room.

Who it suits, and who it does not

The complaints we would examine for suitability: sciatica, a herniated disc, a bulging disc, a slipped disc, a pinched nerve, arthritic change, lumbar disorders, and a long-standing ache in the lower back.

The reader this is written for is an adult in Tracy or the ZIP codes around it, 95376, 95377 and 95304, who is in musculoskeletal pain and wants relief without drugs or surgery.

Some people are not candidates. Some disc problems need imaging or a surgical opinion before anything else, and if that is what yours needs, that is what we will tell you.

Call and describe the complaint, and you will be told which of those two you are.

What the examination decides

The examination decides. Not this website.

If decompression is not the right tool for your back, we will say so, and we will tell you what is.

Your first visit sets out what that examination actually involves, and Dr. Sperbeck is who carries it out.

Traction, not manipulation

For a nervous reader this is the important part, and it is literally true.

Decompression involves no thrust, no twist and no sudden movement. The table does the work, slowly.

If you would rather understand the hands-on option instead, that is explained on chiropractic care.

What decompression will not do

Non-surgical spinal decompression is a drug-free option we use for some disc-related low back pain, and whether it suits you depends on an examination.

That is the whole claim. There is not a larger one behind it.

Some symptoms mean an emergency department rather than this office

  • Progressive weakness in a leg
  • Numbness in the saddle area
  • Any loss of bladder or bowel control
