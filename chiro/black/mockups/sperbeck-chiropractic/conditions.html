Where does it hurt

Spine and back

  • Low back pain the ache or catch that makes bending and sitting hard
  • Upper back and neck pain stiffness between the shoulder blades and up into the neck
  • Slipped disc back pain with a nerve involved
  • Sciatica leg pain that starts in the low back
  • Pinched nerve sharp, local pain that travels
  • Scoliosis a spine that curves sideways

Head and jaw

  • Headache and migraine head pain that often starts in the neck

Arms, hands and feet

  • Shoulder pain trouble lifting or reaching behind you
  • Frozen shoulder a shoulder that has gradually stopped moving
  • Carpal tunnel numbness and tingling in the hand
  • Tennis elbow pain on the outside of the elbow when you grip
  • Plantar fasciitis heel pain that is worst on the first steps of the day

Whole-body and long-term

  • Osteoarthritis joints that ache and stiffen with wear
  • Fibromyalgia widespread pain and tenderness
  • Stress tension that settles in the neck and shoulders
  • Pregnancy pain low back and pelvic pain during pregnancy

What a gentle examination looks like

Nothing is adjusted before it is examined and explained. The first step is a conversation, then an orthopaedic and neurological examination. X-rays are taken only if they are needed.

The technique is low-force. If a joint is guarding, it is not forced.

What is not on this list
