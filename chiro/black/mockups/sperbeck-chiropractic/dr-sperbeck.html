How he adjusts

If you are here because an adjustment somewhere else hurt, or because the idea of one frightens you, this section is the answer.

The techniques are low-force. He explains what he is going to do before he does it. You can stop at any point, and saying so is not a problem.

He is gentle enough to work with older patients, children, and pregnant patients.

The technique itself is set out on chiropractic care.

Who he treats

In the order they actually arrive:

  • People hurt in a motor-vehicle accident
  • People hurt at work
  • People with everyday back, neck and shoulder pain

Also long-term maintenance patients, seniors, pregnant patients and local athletes.

The full index is on conditions, and the non-surgical option for some disc-related pain is spinal decompression.

Working with your medical doctor

Dr. Sperbeck takes your health seriously and will work with your medical doctor, or refer you to an appropriate specialist, if your condition requires it.

He will also tell you when chiropractic is not the right answer for what you have.
