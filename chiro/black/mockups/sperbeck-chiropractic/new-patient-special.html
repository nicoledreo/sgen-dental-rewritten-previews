The offer, in full

What the $39 covers, and what it does not

What it covers
A complete consultation, an examination, and x-rays if needed. X-rays are taken only if the examination indicates them. The "if needed" is not a formality and it is not quietly dropped to make the offer sound bigger.
What it does not
It does not state a treatment fee, and this practice publishes no per-visit rate anywhere. If Dr. Sperbeck recommends treatment, the cost is explained to you before anything is done.
Two steps, not two offers
Call and talk to us for free. Then, when you come in, the first visit is $39. The free consultation is a phone consultation. The $39 is the in-office visit that follows.
The terms
It is a new patient special. That is the only qualifier, and it is the offer's own name.

Who this is for

Who books this

Adults in Tracy and the surrounding ZIP codes, 95376, 95377 and 95304, who are in musculoskeletal pain. Most often that is after a motor-vehicle accident or a work injury. Most want relief without drugs or surgery, and many are nervous about forceful manipulation, often after a bad experience somewhere else.

Also long-term maintenance patients, seniors, pregnant patients and local athletes.

What happens

What the visit includes, in order

  1. A conversation about what is hurting
  2. A physical examination
  3. Digital x-ray, on the same "if needed" basis set out above
  4. An explanation of what was found and what is recommended

The equipment in the room: computerized digital x-ray, hydraulic lift tables, flexion traction for disc injuries, ultrasound, and electrical stimulation.

If you have insurance

How the money works

The $39 is the cash entry point. Separately from it, the practice bills Aetna, Anthem Blue Cross, Blue Shield of California, Cigna, Health Net, Humana and UnitedHealthcare, and is enrolled with Medicare as non-participating.

Bring your plan details and the front desk will explain what to expect. More on insurance and pricing.

No membership, no contract

There is no membership to join and nothing to cancel later. You see the same doctor at every visit rather than whoever is on shift. Insurance is billed rather than refused.

Before you book

Questions people ask

Does the adjustment hurt?

The techniques used here are low-force. Dr. Sperbeck works at your pain level and does not force a joint that is guarding.

What if I am nervous?

Say so. Nothing is done without being explained first, and you can stop at any point.

Do I need x-rays?

Only where they are called for. That is what the "if needed" in the offer means.

How does this work with my insurance?

The $39 is a cash entry point. The practice separately bills seven carriers and will verify your benefits as a courtesy.

Do I need a referral?

No. You can call the office directly.
