Shoulder pain worth getting looked at

We examine the neck before we touch the shoulder

The nerves that serve your shoulder and arm come out of your neck. So the examination starts there and works outward. That order is the reason, not a formality.

What is used, and in what order: hands-on assessment, gentle mobilisation, soft-tissue work and massage therapy, then simple corrective exercises to hold the gain.

What no-force means here, and what we do not do

No fast thrusting technique is used on a shoulder or a neck that is guarding. You set the pain level, at every visit.

05

Where this goes next

Some shoulder problems belong with a medical doctor first

  • A suspected rotator cuff tear
  • A dislocation
  • A shoulder that will not lift after a fall
  • Shoulder pain that comes with chest pain or breathlessness

If that is what you describe, we will say so.
