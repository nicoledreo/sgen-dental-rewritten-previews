New Patient Forms in Tracy

02

The form we have

New Patient Intake Form. That is the one document this practice publishes today, and this page does not pretend there are more.

It is not yet available to download from this site. Call the front desk on (209) 836-5558 and they will have one ready for you, or you can complete it in the office before your appointment.

03

Filling it in before you arrive

Print it, complete it, and bring it with you.

If you cannot print it, say so when you call. The front desk will have one ready, or you can arrive a little early and complete it in the office.

The details people always have to go home for:

  • InsuranceYour insurance card.
  • IDA photo ID.
  • MedicationThe medications you take.
  • ClaimsFor an auto accident or a work injury: the date of the incident, the claim number, and the adjuster's contact.

Those last two are the jobs this practice is most often hired for, by patients across Tracy and the ZIP codes around it, 95376, 95377 and 95304.

04

What happens to what you write

The form asks for health information. It is handled under this practice's privacy policy.

There is no online submission and no secure upload. The practice does have an email address, info@tracychiropractor.com, but ordinary email is not a secure channel for a document full of health history.

So do not email the completed form. Bring it in person, or post it to Sperbeck Chiropractic Corporation, 1770 N. Tracy Blvd. Suite B, Tracy, CA 95376.

The email address is for questions, not for returning this form.

05

The form is not the point

The appointment is

Paperwork done at home buys back the first few minutes of your visit. That is all it does, and it is not a condition of being seen.

If you turn up with nothing filled in, you will still be seen.
