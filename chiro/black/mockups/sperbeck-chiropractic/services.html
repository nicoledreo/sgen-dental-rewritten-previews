Hands-on care

Chiropractic care

The gentle, low-force adjustment. This is the core service, and the page for it is where the technique itself is described.

Spinal decompression

Non-surgical and drug-free, used for some disc-related low back pain.

Massage therapy

Eases the muscle tension around an adjustment so the adjustment holds.

The equipment in the room: hydraulic lift tables, flexion traction for disc injuries, ultrasound, electrical stimulation, and computerised digital x-ray.

Supporting care

Kept deliberately separate from the hands-on work above, because it does a different job. Dr. Sperbeck is the one who spots what needs correcting.

  • Corrective exercises Simple movements taught to you, to hold the result between visits.
  • Nutritional counseling Practical guidance on diet and vitamins.
  • Lifestyle advice Chiropractic care and lifestyle guidance on posture, sleep and work habits.

How a service is chosen

An examination comes first. The service follows from what it finds, not from a package decided before you walked in.

Non-surgical spinal decompression is a drug-free option we use for some disc-related low back pain. Whether it suits you is settled by examining you, not by asking for it.

If what is found sits outside chiropractic scope, you are told so and referred on.
