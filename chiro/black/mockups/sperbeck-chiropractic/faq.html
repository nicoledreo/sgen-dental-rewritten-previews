02

Being adjusted

Does it hurt?

Usually not. Dr. Sperbeck works at your pain level and uses low-force techniques, so there is no sudden or forceful movement. He explains what he is about to do before he does it, and if something is uncomfortable you say so and he stops.

Will you twist my neck?

Not without telling you first, and not at all if you would rather he did not. The methods here are low-force. You can decline any part of it and still be treated.

What if I have had a bad experience with a chiropractor before?

That is who this practice is set up for. Say so when you call. Nothing is done without being explained first, and you can stop at any point in the visit.

Can you treat an older patient, a child, or someone pregnant?

Yes. Dr. Sperbeck is gentle enough to work with older patients, children and pregnant patients.

03

The visit, and the money

Do I need a referral?

No. Chiropractors are portal-of-entry providers, so you can call this office directly and book without going through anyone else first.

Do you take my insurance?

The practice bills Aetna, Anthem Blue Cross, Blue Shield of California, Cigna, Health Net, Humana and UnitedHealthcare, and is enrolled with Medicare as non-participating. See insurance and pricing.

What does the $39 special include?

A complete comprehensive consultation and examination, and x-rays if they are needed. It does not mention an adjustment, and this site does not imply that it includes one. See the new patient special.

Will I have to keep coming forever?

No. The goal is to stabilise the condition. After that, continuing is your choice, and you will not be sold a long plan on day one.
