What actually happens, in order.

1

We listen

What happened, when it started, what makes it worse and what you have already tried. Fifteen minutes and we do not interrupt. If you were in a collision, this is also where we take the claim details.

2

We examine

Posture and gait, range of motion, orthopaedic and neurological screening, palpation of the affected segments. Imaging only if the examination gives us a reason to order it.

3

We explain

What we found, on a screen you can see, in language without jargon. You will leave knowing what is wrong, roughly how long it takes, and exactly what it costs.

4

We treat

Your first adjustment the same visit if it is appropriate, plus two or three things to do at home. Some people leave with a referral instead, and that is a legitimate outcome.

Three ways people pay us.

Through your insurance

We bill Anthem, Aetna, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, Health Plan of Nevada and Medicare directly. Bring your card to the first visit and we will verify benefits before you are treated, so you know your copay in the room rather than in a letter three weeks later.

Through an auto claim

If you were in a collision you pay nothing at the visit. We bill the at-fault carrier or your own med-pay coverage and produce the documentation your adjuster or attorney needs. Bring your claim number if you have one — if you do not, we will help you open it.

Cash, at the published price

No insurance is genuinely fine and it is not a worse deal here. $59 for the exam, $55 a visit after that, $85 for an extended visit. Those are the numbers on this page and they are the numbers at the desk.

Before you come in.

What should I bring?
Photo ID, your insurance card if you have one, and a claim number if you are here after a collision. If you have had imaging or surgery on the area, bring whatever records you have. Wear something you can move in — we will be checking range of motion.
Do I need a referral?
No. Nevada is a direct-access state, so you can walk in or book without a physician referral. A handful of insurance plans still want one for reimbursement; we check that during benefit verification and tell you before you are billed, not after.
Can I really just walk in for the first visit?
Yes, and plenty of people do. We hold same-day capacity every day. Mornings and just after 5pm are busiest — if the wait is more than about twenty minutes we will tell you honestly and offer a time later that day rather than have you sit.
How long until I feel a difference?
Most people notice a change within two to four visits. If you have had nothing by visit four, that is information — we reassess rather than continue, and sometimes that means referring you on. Chronic cases of many years take longer than a fresh injury.
Will you try to sign me up for a plan?
No. There is no prepaid package, no contract and no discount for buying visits ahead. You get a written estimate after the exam and you can stop whenever you like, including after the first visit.
Is parking difficult?
No. There is a free ground-level lot directly at the door off West Sahara — no garage, no validation, no ticket. Step-free entry, an accessible restroom, and a low-height adjustment table if you need one.

Tell us what is going on.

We answer booking requests within one business hour during opening times. In pain right now? Call us instead, or just come in — we keep same-day slots back for walk-ins.
