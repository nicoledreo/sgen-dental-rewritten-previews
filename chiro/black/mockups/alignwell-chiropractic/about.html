Dr. Elena Marsh, DC

Doctor of Chiropractic · Webster-certified · practising in the valley since 2009

Elena trained at Palmer West and spent four years in a high-volume Henderson practice before opening Alignwell in 2009. She left because the model there rewarded visit counts rather than outcomes, and she wanted to run somewhere that published its prices and told people when they did not need care.

She treats the full range but most of her week is post-accident work — the valley produces a steady stream of whiplash and low-back injury, and she has spent seventeen years learning how to document it properly for insurers and how to get people moving again without dragging it out. She is Webster-certified for prenatal care and sees a lot of the neighbourhood's children.

What being independent actually changes.

Why are your prices on the website?
Because the alternative is a conversation at the desk where you have already committed. The exam is $59, a standard adjustment is $55, an extended visit with soft-tissue work is $85. Those are the numbers whether you read them here or hear them in the room.
Do you sell treatment packages?
No. There is no prepaid block, no contract and no discount for buying ahead. After your exam you get a written estimate of how many visits we think you need — usually four to twelve — and you can stop at any point.
What if chiropractic is not the right answer for me?
Then we will say so at the first visit and refer you on, and you will not be charged for treatment you did not need. Roughly one in twelve people who come through the door leaves with a referral rather than an adjustment.
Do you speak Spanish?
Yes — Nadia handles intake in English and Spanish, and Dr. Okafor consults in Spanish. Se habla español todos los días.
