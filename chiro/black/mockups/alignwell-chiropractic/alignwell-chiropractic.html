Forty-five minutes. Most of it is us listening.

Nobody gets adjusted at Alignwell before we understand what is going on. If we do not think chiropractic is the right answer, we will say so and point you somewhere better.

01

We listen

What happened, when it started, what makes it worse, and what you have already tried. Fifteen minutes, uninterrupted.

02

We examine

Posture, range of motion, orthopaedic and neurological screening. Imaging only if the exam actually calls for it.

03

We explain

What we found, in plain language, on a screen you can see. Including how long it should take and what it will cost.

04

We treat

Usually your first adjustment the same visit, plus two or three things to do at home. No twelve-month plan, no upfront package.

We use a segment model to show you exactly where the restriction is. Most people have never been shown their own spine before.
