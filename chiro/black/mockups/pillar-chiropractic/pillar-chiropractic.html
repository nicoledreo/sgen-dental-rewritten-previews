The pattern we see most

You have been managing it. For years.

Most people who walk in have already tried the obvious things. The pain gets quiet for a while, then comes back in the same place. That is not bad luck — it usually means the cause was never found, only the symptom was.

  • Painkillers that work until they stop working
  • A scan that came back clear, with no plan attached
  • Stretches from the internet that help for an afternoon
  • Being told to rest, then to move, then to rest again

Your first visit

Ninety minutes. No guesswork.

The thing that stops most people booking is not knowing what happens. So here it is, in order. Nothing gets adjusted until step three.

01

History, properly taken

Twenty minutes on what happened, what you have already tried, and what your week actually looks like. Most of the answer is in here.

02

Orthopaedic and neurological exam

Range, strength, reflexes, and the specific tests for your presentation. If you have imaging, we read it with you in the room.

03

Findings and a written plan

What we found, what is causing it, how many visits we expect, and what it costs. In writing, before treatment starts.

04

First treatment

Adjustment plus soft-tissue work if it is indicated, then two or three things to do at home. If we are not the right people, we will say so and refer you.
