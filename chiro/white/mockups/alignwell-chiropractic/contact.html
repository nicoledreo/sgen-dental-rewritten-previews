7250 W Sahara Ave, Suite 140 · Las Vegas, NV 89117

We are on the ground floor of the low stucco building on the north side of West Sahara, just past South Buffalo Drive heading west. Suite 140 is the second door along the covered walkway — the one with the planters.

The lot is open, free and directly outside; there is no garage, no ticket and nothing to validate. Two accessible bays sit nearest the entrance, the entry is step-free, and there is an accessible restroom and a low-height adjustment table for anyone who needs one.

The RTC 206 stops on Sahara at Buffalo, a two-minute walk east. If you are coming from the 215 take the Sahara exit and head east for about a mile and a half.

Three ways, depending on how urgent it is.

Just walk in

We hold same-day capacity every day we are open. Best times are mid-morning and mid-afternoon; the busiest are opening and just after five. Last walk-in is taken thirty minutes before we close.

Call us

(702) 555-0148 reaches the front desk, not a call centre. If you are ringing after a collision, say so — those get triaged to the same day wherever we possibly can.

Book online

Use the form below and we will confirm within one business hour during opening times. Requests that come in overnight are answered first thing the next morning.

Tell us what is going on.

The more you tell us here, the less you repeat in the room. If you were in a collision, include the claim number and we will have the paperwork started before you arrive.

Practical questions about getting here.

Are you open on Saturday?
Yes, 9am to 2pm, and it is one of our busier sessions — walk-ins are welcome but arriving before noon usually means a shorter wait. We are closed Sundays.
What is the parking situation?
A free open lot directly outside the suite, no garage and no validation needed. Two accessible bays sit closest to the entrance and the walkway in is step-free.
Do you speak Spanish?
Yes. Intake and front desk are handled in English and Spanish daily, and Dr. Okafor consults in Spanish. Se habla español todos los días.
How quickly can I be seen after a car accident?
Same day in almost every case, and within 24 hours otherwise. Call rather than use the form if the collision was recent — the first 72 hours matter both for how you recover and for how the claim is documented.
