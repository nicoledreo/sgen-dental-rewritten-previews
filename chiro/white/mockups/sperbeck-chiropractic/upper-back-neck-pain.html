Why neck pain travels into the shoulder and arm

Your head weighs roughly fourteen to sixteen pounds. Your neck holds it up and moves it all day.

So those joints and muscles fatigue, and they get injured.

The nerves that supply your arm exit through the neck. That is why an arm that aches, tingles or goes numb can trace back to a neck problem rather than a shoulder one.

Everyday causes are usually ordinary ones. Posture, your desk, your driving position, how you sleep, stress, and changes in the discs.

  • ReportedNumbness, tingling, coldness and aching.
  • MovementA neck that will only turn one way.
