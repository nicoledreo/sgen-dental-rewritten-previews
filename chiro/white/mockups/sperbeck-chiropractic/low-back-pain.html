What is going on in a painful low back

Your lower back carries most of your body's load. It also bends, twists and leans in every direction you move.

That is the point of examining you before deciding anything.

  • InjuryThat combination is why so much turns up here. Sprained ligaments, disc injuries and strained muscles all present as low back pain.
  • LoadSo do the slower causes. How you sit, how long you sit, how you lift, and how much weight your frame is carrying.
  • ReferralPain in one spot does not always start there. A problem lower down the chain can refer pain upward, and the reverse is also true.
  • Who comes inMost people who come in with this live in Tracy and the ZIP codes around it, 95376, 95377 and 95304. Many arrive after a car accident or a work injury, wanting relief without drugs or surgery.

How Dr. Sperbeck works on a low back

  • He checks how it moves first

    He checks how each segment actually moves before he does anything to it. He works at your pain level, and he does not force a joint that is guarding.

  • The equipment is on the premises

    If a disc is the suspected driver, flexion traction is on the premises. So are hydraulic lift tables, ultrasound, electrical stimulation and digital x-ray.

What we will not claim, and when we refer you on

What we aim to do

We aim to address what is causing the pain rather than only quieting the symptom. Where your care belongs with someone else, we say so.

Dr. Sperbeck takes your health seriously and will work with your medical doctor, or refer you to an appropriate specialist, if your condition requires it.

Seek urgent medical care now

  • Sudden weakness in a leg
  • Numbness in the saddle area
  • Loss of bladder or bowel control

Some things are not a reason to book an adjustment. Any of the above is a reason to seek urgent medical care now.
