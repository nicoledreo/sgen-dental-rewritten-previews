What osteoarthritis pain does through the day

In the hip and knee it often shows as a shooting pain through the hip, the groin and the thigh.

The knee pain usually arrives first. The hip pain gets recognised later, sometimes much later.

It tends to peak in colder weather, and after activity.

03

Relief, not repair

Here is the honest version, in plain words.

We use ultrasound and gentle manipulation to help manage osteoarthritis pain. Stretching and massage work sit alongside them as comfort measures.

What this page will not suggest

Osteoarthritis is a long-term joint condition. Care manages it. Care does not reverse it. We cannot restore worn cartilage, and this page will not suggest otherwise.

What we can offer is straight forward answers, quality care and no nonsense, which is how one of this practice's own patients put it.
