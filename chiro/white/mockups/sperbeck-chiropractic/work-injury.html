The work injuries we actually see in Tracy

Back and neck complaints are the most common thing walked into this office from a job.

Most of them do not come from one dramatic moment. They come from repetition: the same lift, the same reach, the same seated position, day after day, until it stops settling overnight.

  • BackA low back that will not release after a shift.
  • NeckNeck and shoulder ache from driving or screen work.
  • WristRepetitive strain in the wrist and forearm, which is often carpal tunnel.
03

The same doctor, every visit

A work complaint develops over months. It needs someone who remembers what it looked like in week one.

Here that is one named doctor, in one Tracy office, by appointment rather than a queue, with thirty years of the same hands.

The care: examination first, then gentle low-force adjusting, soft-tissue and massage work, ultrasound or electrical stimulation where indicated, and corrective exercises plus practical work-habit changes so the gain survives the next shift. More on lifestyle advice.

Where this goes next

Some symptoms need urgent medical care first

  • Numbness or weakness that is getting worse
  • A hand that drops things
  • Back pain with fever
  • Any loss of bladder or bowel control

Any of those means urgent medical care rather than booking here.
