02

What a slipped disc actually is

"Slipped disc" is the everyday name for a disc that has bulged or herniated between two vertebrae.

When the outer wall of the disc tears, the material inside can irritate the nearby nerve and make it swell. That is why the pain is so often felt in the leg or the arm rather than in the back or neck itself.

What contributes: posture, smoking, sitting still for long stretches, poor nutrition, load, and age.

Two things worth knowing before you worry. Many disc problems settle with conservative care. And what shows on an image does not always match what a person actually feels.
