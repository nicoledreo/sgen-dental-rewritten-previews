Frozen Shoulder Care in Tracy

Reaching for a seat belt. A top shelf. The back of your own head. Those are the things that have become hard.

We are the gentle, no-force chiropractor in Tracy for people who are nervous about being adjusted, which a frozen shoulder makes entirely reasonable.

02

What frozen shoulder does, in the order it does it

Pain in that shoulder comes first.

Then the range narrows. Not all at once, but far enough that ordinary reaches stop being available.

It is most common between about forty and seventy, and more often in women.

The causes we can honestly name are mechanical: an injury, misuse, a long period of not using the arm, and recovery from upper-torso surgery.

03

Before we touch the shoulder

Some of what causes a frozen shoulder is not ours to treat.

If you have had a cardiac event, or shoulder or breast surgery, see your physician first. Then bring us what they say.

Dr. Sperbeck takes your health seriously and will work with your medical doctor, or refer you to an appropriate specialist, if your condition requires it.

What does belong with us, at a pace you set:

  • In officeIn-office physiotherapy.
  • Hands onGentle mobilisation.
  • At homeA graded home-exercise plan.
