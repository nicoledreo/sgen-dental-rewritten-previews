Plantar Fasciitis in Tracy

That stab in the heel when your foot hits the floor in the morning. It eases as you keep walking, then comes back after you sit down.

We are the gentle, no-force chiropractor in Tracy for people who are nervous about being adjusted. This page tells you what we do and what we do not do.

02

What it is, and who tends to get it

The plantar fascia is the thick band running along the bottom of your foot, from the heel bone to the toes.

The pain is a stab at the heel with the first steps of the day. It fades as you walk. It is usually one foot, sometimes both.

Who tends to get it: runners, people on their feet all day in unsupportive shoes, pregnant patients, and people carrying extra weight.

The conservative care we actually provide

04

What we do not provide

We treat plantar fasciitis conservatively. If your case needs care we do not provide, we will tell you and refer you.

To be concrete, this office does not:

  • prescribe medication
  • perform injections
  • operate
  • provide the device-based procedures some foot clinics offer
  • make or dispense custom orthotics

Some things belong with your medical doctor or a podiatrist first:

  • Numbness
  • A sudden pop with immediate swelling
  • Heel pain that started with a fall
