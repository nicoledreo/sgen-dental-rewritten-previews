02

Why it is rarely about tennis

Despite the name, any job, sport or household task that repeats a forearm and wrist movement can cause it.

Around here that means the trades, warehouse and line work, driving, and the ordinary strain that fills this appointment book alongside collision and work-injury cases.

The pain starts at the outer elbow and travels into the forearm and wrist. The weakness shows up holding things, turning doorknobs, and shaking hands.

It is most common roughly between thirty and fifty, though it happens at any age.

03

Examining the elbow, the shoulder and the neck together

Pain felt at the elbow can be driven by how the shoulder and the neck are moving. So all three get examined before anything is recommended.

  • ElbowWhere the pain is felt.
  • ShoulderHow it is moving, and what it is compensating for. See shoulder pain.
  • NeckThe nerves serving the arm leave here.

The handling is gentle and low-force. Alongside it: soft-tissue work through the forearm, and a graded stretching and strengthening plan using corrective exercises.
