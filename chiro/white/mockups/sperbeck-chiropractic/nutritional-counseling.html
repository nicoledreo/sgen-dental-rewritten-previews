Nutritional Counseling in Tracy, CA

Practical guidance about eating and drinking that supports the care you are already having here.

One of the six services this practice provides.

Call (209) 836-5558

What this covers

Around an injury

What to eat and drink while you are sore.

Hydration

It matters more than most people expect when muscle is guarding.

Weight off a painful joint

Where that is relevant to you, and only where it is.

What this does not cover

We give food and habit guidance that supports the care we provide. We do not treat medical conditions through diet.

That is the boundary, and there is nothing behind it.

Who gives the advice, and its limits

Dr. Charles Sperbeck, D.C. gives it, in the room, as part of a visit. More about him on Dr. Sperbeck.

He is not a dietitian. This is guidance, and it does not replace what your own doctor tells you.

He will work with your medical doctor, or refer you to an appropriate specialist, if your condition requires it.

Check with your medical doctor before changing your diet

If you are pregnant, if you have diabetes or kidney disease, or if you take prescription medication.

Supplements

If you have a question about supplements, raise it at a visit and Dr. Sperbeck will answer it honestly.

This page names no product, no brand and no dosage, and it makes no claim about what any supplement does.

What the conversation is actually like

It happens inside a normal visit. It takes minutes, not an hour.

You leave with two or three specific things to change. Not a diet plan, and not a printed programme.

No pressure, and no programme to buy

If you would rather not talk about food, we will not raise it again.

There is nothing to sign up for here.

Before you come in at all, the consultation over the phone is free and there is no pressure attached to it. The $39 is the in-office visit that follows.

What it costs

No fee for nutritional guidance is published on this site, because the practice has not set one.

The $39 new patient special is the entry point to being seen. For anything further, see insurance and pricing or ask the front desk.

What a first appointment involves is on the visit walkthrough, and the questions page covers the rest.
