Your First Chiropractic Visit in Tracy

We are the gentle, no-force chiropractor in Tracy for people who are nervous about being adjusted.

This page tells you exactly what happens on visit one, in order.

Call (209) 836-5558
02

What actually happens, in order

  • Your paperwork is already done

    If you printed it at home. See the form.

  • You talk to Dr. Sperbeck

    About what happened and what is worrying you.

  • A detailed history, then an examination

    An orthopedic and neurological examination.

  • Diagnostic spinal x-rays, if the examination indicates them

    Not everyone needs one.

  • He tells you what he found

    And what he proposes, in plain words, before he touches you.

  • If you agree, a gentle low-force adjustment

    Only if you agree.

Equipment that may be used: hydraulic lift tables, flexion traction for disc injuries, ultrasound, electrical stimulation, and computerized digital x-ray.

He explains what he is about to do before he does it. You can tell him to stop.

He is gentle enough to work with older patients, children and pregnant patients.
