Practical questions

How quickly can I be seen?
Within five days across the three clinics, and often sooner if you are flexible on location. The booking screen shows real availability, so what you see is what is actually free.
What should I bring to the first appointment?
Anything you already have — scan reports, GP letters, a list of medication — and clothing you can move in. Allow the full ninety minutes; it is not a thirty-minute appointment that finishes early.
Can I change or cancel a booking?
Yes, up to 24 hours before, from the link in your confirmation email or by calling the clinic. Inside 24 hours we ask for the appointment fee, because the slot cannot usually be refilled.
Is there parking and step-free access?
Northgate and Camden Row have step-free entrances and ground-floor treatment rooms. Riverside has three steps at the front with a ramp available — call ahead and we will have it out for you. Parking is on-street at all three.
