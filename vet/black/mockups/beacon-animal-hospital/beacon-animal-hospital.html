Is this an emergency?

If you see
any of
these,
come in now.

These are the signs that should not wait until morning. There are others — when in doubt, call us and we will tell you.

Bleeding that will not stop

Pressure held for five minutes and it is still going.

Trouble breathing

Open-mouth breathing in a cat, or gums turning pale or blue.

Seizure or collapse

Any seizure, or a sudden inability to stand or walk.

Hit by a car

Even if they get up and seem fine. Internal injury hides.

Ate something toxic

Chocolate, xylitol, grapes, lilies, antifreeze, medication.

Swollen, hard abdomen

Especially with retching that brings nothing up. This is minutes-critical.

Questions people ask at 3am

Straight answers.

Are you really open right now?+
Yes. We are staffed 24 hours a day, every day of the year, including public holidays. There is a veterinarian physically on site — not on call from home — at every hour.
Do I need an appointment for an emergency?+
No. Walk straight in. Appointments exist only for scheduled, non-urgent care. If you are worried enough to be reading this, come in.
What will it cost?+
You will see a written estimate before any treatment begins, and nothing proceeds without your approval. Emergency consultation starts at $145. We accept major pet insurers and offer payment plans through CareCredit.
Can I stay with my pet?+
Yes, and we would rather you did. You are welcome in the treatment area for as much of your pet's care as you want to see. Animals settle faster when their person is in the room.
Do you treat rabbits, birds and reptiles?+
We do. Exotic species are seen around the clock by clinicians trained specifically in avian and exotic emergency medicine. Please call ahead if you can, so we can prepare the right equipment.
My regular vet is closed — will you send them the records?+
Automatically, the next working morning. Your primary vet receives a full discharge summary, imaging and lab results so nothing has to be repeated.
