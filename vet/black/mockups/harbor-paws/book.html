Before you book.

How quickly can I be seen?
We hold same-day slots for pets that are unwell. Routine wellness and dental appointments are usually available within the week. Anything urgent should go to the 24/7 line rather than this form.
Do you take pet insurance?
We work with all major pet insurers. We ask for payment at the time of the visit and send the itemised claim documentation the same day, so reimbursement is not held up by our paperwork. For planned surgery we can prepare a written estimate in advance.
My pet is anxious in waiting rooms.
Tell us when you book. We will note it, give you a quieter slot where we can, and bring you straight through to a room rather than have you wait out front. For cats we can also advise on getting the carrier to be less of a fight.
