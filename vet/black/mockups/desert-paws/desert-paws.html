Things that only matter if you live out here.

Most pet advice is written for places that aren't 108° in July. This is the Mojave-specific version we give every new client.

Heat safety

The 10am rule

From May to September, walks happen before 10am or after 8pm. Dogs cool by panting, and 108° air cools nothing. Shade and water are not optional — they're the whole plan.

Paw protection

The seven-second test

Press the back of your hand to the pavement. If you can't hold it for seven seconds, neither can their paws. Asphalt hits 140° when the air is only 95°. Grass, booties, or wait.

Rattlesnakes

Leash, vaccine, and a plan

Mojave greens are active dawn and dusk, spring through fall. Keep dogs leashed on trails, ask us about the rattlesnake vaccine, and if you're bitten — call us on the way, don't wait.

The things people call and ask.

Do you actually take walk-ins? +
Yes, for urgent problems — injuries, sudden illness, something swallowed. We hold same-day slots open every day for exactly this. Call ahead if you can so we can prepare, but if you just show up with a sick pet, we will see you. Routine wellness visits are by appointment so nobody waits three hours behind an emergency.
Which exotics do you treat? +
Reptiles (bearded dragons, geckos, snakes, tortoises), birds (parrots, conures, cockatiels, budgies) and pocket pets (rabbits, guinea pigs, rats, hamsters, ferrets). We do not treat venomous species or large livestock. If you're unsure, call and ask — we will tell you honestly if you'd be better off with a specialist.
What will this cost me? +
The exam fee is published: $59 standard, $95 urgent care, $39 for a new client's first visit. Everything beyond the exam is quoted in writing before we start, and if the plan changes mid-visit we call you before doing anything that changes the number.
¿Hablan español? +
Sí. Dos de nuestros veterinarios y tres técnicos hablan español con fluidez, todos los días que estamos abiertos — no hace falta pedir cita especial ni traer traductor. Los presupuestos también se entregan por escrito en español si lo prefiere.
What if something happens after you close? +
We are a day practice, not a 24-hour hospital — we will never pretend otherwise. Our urgent line at (702) 555-0199 is answered during open hours. Outside those hours the line gives you the two nearest 24-hour emergency hospitals with directions, and we follow up the next morning to pick your pet's care back up.
Can I keep seeing the same doctor? +
Yes, and we'd prefer it. Tell us who you saw last time when you book and we'll put you back on their schedule wherever it's possible. For same-day urgent visits you get whichever doctor is on the floor — but they'll have your full history, not a summary.
