Getting here

Parking, access, and the after-hours door.

Parking

Twelve spaces directly outside, including two accessible bays by the entrance. On-street parking on Wharf Road is free after 18:00.

Step-free access

The entrance, both waiting areas, all consulting rooms and the accessible toilet are step-free. Trolleys are available for large dogs that cannot walk in.

After hours

Same door as the day clinic. Ring the bell to the right of the entrance and a member of the overnight team will come to you.

Ask us something.

For anything urgent please call rather than write — the 24/7 line is answered in the building. For everything else, this reaches the practice team and we reply the same working day.
