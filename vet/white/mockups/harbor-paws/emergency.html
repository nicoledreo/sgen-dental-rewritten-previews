When to come in

Things that should not wait until morning.

Breathing trouble

Open-mouth breathing in a cat, laboured or noisy breathing in a dog, gums that look pale, blue or grey. This is the one to never sit on.

Cannot stand, or collapsed

Sudden weakness, dragging a limb, a fall, or being hit by a vehicle — even if they seem fine afterwards. Internal injury is often quiet at first.

Swallowed something

Medication, chocolate, grapes, xylitol, antifreeze, a toy, a sock. Bring the packaging if you have it. Do not try to make them sick unless a vet tells you to.

Repeated vomiting or retching

Especially in a deep-chested dog with a swollen, tight abdomen and unproductive retching. That combination is a genuine emergency.

Straining in the litter tray

A male cat straining without producing urine is an emergency within hours, not days. This one is easy to mistake for constipation.

You are simply worried

You know them better than any list does. If something is off and you cannot say why, call. We would far rather tell you it can wait until Tuesday.

What happens when you arrive.

1

Straight to triage

A veterinary nurse assesses your pet on arrival. The sickest animal in the building is seen first, which occasionally means a wait for less urgent cases.

2

A plan and an estimate

Before anything beyond stabilising happens, a veterinarian talks you through what they think is going on and what it will cost.

3

Updates, not silence

If your pet stays with us, you get a call from the veterinarian treating them — not a message passed through reception.

Emergency questions.

Is someone really there at night?
Yes. A veterinarian is physically on site overnight, not on call from home. You do not need an appointment and you do not need to be an existing client to be seen in an emergency.
Do I need to call first?
It helps — it lets us prepare the room and, if needed, the surgical team before you arrive. But it is not a requirement. If your pet is in serious trouble, drive, and have a passenger call on the way.
What does an emergency visit cost?
The out-of-hours consultation fee is quoted to you on arrival, and nothing beyond immediate stabilisation happens without a written estimate you have agreed to. We work with all major pet insurers and send claim documentation the same day.

If you are unsure, call. That is what the line is for.
