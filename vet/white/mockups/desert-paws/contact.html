Finding us, parking, and what to bring.

Where we are

8420 W. Warm Springs Rd, Suite 110, Las Vegas NV 89113 — the low sand-coloured building set back from Warm Springs, just west of Durango. We are the suite with the agave planting by the door.

Parking & access

Free lot directly in front, including two accessible bays. Step-free entrance. In summer, call from the car and we will bring you straight through rather than have your pet wait on hot asphalt.

What to bring

Any previous records you have, the food or supplement labels if diet is involved, and for reptiles a photo of the enclosure — temperatures and lighting explain most reptile problems we see.

After hours

We are a day practice. Outside open hours our line names the two nearest 24-hour emergency hospitals with directions, and we follow up the next morning to pick your pet's care back up.

Before you come in.

How quickly will you reply to this form?
During open hours, usually within the hour. If you send it after we close, you will hear from us when we open. For anything that cannot wait that long, call (702) 555-0199 instead.
Can I request a specific doctor?
Yes — put the name in the notes field and we will book you onto their schedule wherever possible. For same-day urgent visits you get whichever doctor is on the floor, but they will have your pet's full history.
Do you take payment plans or CareCredit?
We accept CareCredit and can put larger surgical estimates on a written payment plan. Ask before the procedure rather than after — it is a normal request and we would rather sort it up front.
