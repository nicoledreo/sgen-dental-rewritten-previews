For Parents

Questions Parents Often Ask

Everything you need to feel confident before your child's first visit.

What age should my child first visit?
We recommend a first visit by your child's first birthday, or within six months of their first tooth appearing. Early, friendly visits help build comfort and catch anything before it becomes a worry.
How do you manage dental anxiety?
We use a gentle "show, tell, do" approach, plenty of extra time, sensory-friendly pacing, and lots of encouragement. Parents stay right beside their child, and we never rush. Many nervous kids leave excited for the next visit.
Do you accept walk-ins?
We see scheduled visits to keep wait times short and stress low for little ones, but we always keep room for dental emergencies. Call us and we'll do everything we can to fit your child in the same day.
What if my child is scared?
That's completely normal, and we're ready for it. We go at your child's pace — sometimes a first visit is just a happy hello and a chair ride. There's zero pressure, and every small step of bravery gets celebrated.
Do you have after-school appointments?
Yes! We reserve plenty of after-school and school-hours slots so visits fit around your family's day. You can pick one right in our online booking above.
