Questions & Answers

Answers for Every Family

Honest answers to the things families ask us most.

Do you offer payment plans?
Yes! We offer interest-free payment plans and work with you to find a plan that fits your budget. Just ask Diego at the front desk — no judgment, ever.
Do you accept walk-ins?
Absolutely. Walk-ins are always welcome at every location, including for dental emergencies. We'll do our best to see you the same day.
What insurance do you accept?
We accept most major dental insurance plans and will help you understand your coverage before any treatment. No insurance? Ask about our uninsured family discounts.
What languages does your team speak?
Our entire team is fluent in both English and Spanish — dentists, hygienists, and front desk. Se habla español en cada visita.
Is the clinic good for kids and seniors?
Yes — we care for every generation. From a toddler's first visit to a grandparent's dentures, we tailor each visit to be gentle, patient, and welcoming.
