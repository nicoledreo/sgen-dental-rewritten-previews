Good to Know

Questions, answered.

Do I need a referral to book?
No. Book a consultation directly and we'll assess what you actually need — often less than you'd expect.
Will whitening look obviously fake?
Not with us. We calibrate shade against your skin tone and lip colour, and we rarely chase the brightest end of the scale. The aim is refined, not artificial.
How long do veneers take?
Typically two to three visits: design and preparation, then fitting. Each set is hand-finished in proportion to your face, so we never rush the finish.
Is the studio suitable for nervous patients?
Especially so. The rooms are quiet and unhurried, appointments are unrushed, and nothing happens before it's clearly explained.
Do you offer payment plans?
Yes — most treatments can be staged over interest-free instalments. We'll lay out the full cost in writing before anything begins.
