Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need before your first appointment — and who to ask if you need more.

How do I book an appointment?
You can book online through our website or call your nearest clinic directly. Our reception team will help you find a time that suits your schedule.
Do you accept new patients?
Yes — Bright Smile Dental warmly welcomes new patients at all locations. We'll make your first visit simple and comfortable.
Do you see children?
Yes, we offer gentle and friendly dental care for patients of all ages, with a focus on helping children feel at ease and build healthy habits.
What cosmetic services do you offer?
We offer teeth whitening, veneers, orthodontics, and more. Consult with our team for personalised recommendations tailored to your smile goals.
Do you offer emergency appointments?
Yes. For urgent dental care, contact your nearest clinic directly and we'll do our best to see you as soon as possible.
How often should I have a dental check-up?
Most patients benefit from a check-up every six months. Your dentist will advise on the right schedule based on your individual needs.
Do you offer payment plans?
Please confirm with your clinic for current payment and financing options available — our reception team will be happy to walk you through them.
