FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your visit.

Do I need an appointment?
Yes. Appointments are recommended for all eye exams and consultations to ensure dedicated time with your doctor.
Do you accept walk-in patients?
Walk-in availability varies by location. Booking in advance is always recommended.
What is included in a comprehensive eye exam?
A complete exam includes a vision assessment, full eye health evaluation, and a review of any concerns or symptoms — plus a discussion of recommended treatments or corrective options.
Do you see children?
Yes. American Eye provides eye care for patients of all ages, including children and seniors.
Do you offer LASIK?
We provide LASIK consultations and guide patients through all available vision correction options.
How often should I get an eye exam?
Most adults should have a comprehensive eye exam every one to two years. Your doctor will recommend a schedule based on your individual needs.
