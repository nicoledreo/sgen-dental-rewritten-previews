Lens education

The right lens does the heavy lifting.

Every prescription is built on a lens. Here's what each upgrade actually does — explained plainly, recommended honestly at your exam.

Blue-light filtering

A lens coating that reduces the high-energy blue light from screens and LED lighting — the kind associated with digital eye strain after long days.

  • Eases tired, dry eyes during screen-heavy days
  • Can support more comfortable evenings before sleep
  • Adds to any prescription, clear or tinted
Ask about it at your exam →

Transitions® adaptive

One lens that's clear indoors and darkens automatically in sunlight — so a single pair handles your whole day without swapping to sunglasses.

  • Clear inside, tinted outside — fully automatic
  • Blocks 100% of UVA & UVB rays
  • Available in multiple tint colors
Ask about it at your exam →

Anti-glare (anti-reflective)

A near-invisible coating that cuts reflections off the lens surface — sharper vision, less halo around headlights, and clearer eye contact on camera.

  • Reduces glare for night driving
  • Smudge- & scratch-resistant top layer
  • Your eyes show clearly on video calls
Ask about it at your exam →

Progressive lenses

Near, intermediate, and distance vision in one seamless lens — no visible line, no swapping readers. The modern replacement for bifocals.

  • One pair for reading, screens & distance
  • No visible line across the lens
  • Custom-fitted to your frame & eyes
Ask about it at your exam →
