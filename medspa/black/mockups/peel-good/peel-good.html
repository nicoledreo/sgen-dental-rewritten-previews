Honest Answers

Everything you're wondering.

Peels can feel intimidating. We'd rather over-explain than have you guess. Still unsure? Bring it to your free mapping session.

Most clients describe a controlled tingle, not pain. Our Glow Peel has zero downtime; deeper peels may leave you pink and flaky for a few days, which we walk you through in advance so there are no surprises. Your depth is always matched to a recovery window you're comfortable with.

Yes — with the right protocol. We use specific acids and conservative depths proven safe for deeper Fitzpatrick skin types, and we patch-test and pre-condition where needed. Our clinicians are trained to minimise the risk of post-inflammatory pigmentation, which is exactly why mapping comes first.

A single Glow Peel gives an immediate fresh-faced glow. For pigmentation and texture, most clients see meaningful change across a 3-session course spaced 2–4 weeks apart. We'll set honest, individual expectations at your mapping — never an open-ended package.

A peel resurfaces; post-care protects and extends. Daily SPF is non-negotiable — without it, sun undoes pigment work fast. Beyond that, we only recommend the few products that genuinely support your result. Buy them from us or bring your own; we'll tell you honestly what's worth it.

Every price is listed on the menu above, per session — no consultation fee, no surprise add-ons. Skin mapping is always free. If a course is right for you, we'll quote the full course up front so you can decide with the whole picture.
