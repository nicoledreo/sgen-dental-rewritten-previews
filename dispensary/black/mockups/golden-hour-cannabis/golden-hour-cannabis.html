Our shop

Built for the hour after the heat breaks.

We opened on Mesa Verde Road in 2021 with one rule: nothing on the shelf we could not explain. That meant driving out to the farms, sitting with the growers, and turning down a lot of product that looked great in a photo and tasted like nothing.

The shop itself is oak, brass and a lot of west-facing glass, because the best hour in the desert is the one right before the sun goes. That is when the room turns gold, the counter fills up, and the whole thing makes sense.

Twelve growers. No mystery jars. Everything tested, everything labeled, everything we would take home ourselves.

Before you come in

Questions worth answering.

That is the whole idea. Build your order on the live menu, choose a pickup time, and we will have it bagged and labeled at the express counter. Most orders are ready in about fifteen minutes. You pay in store when you collect.

A valid government-issued photo ID showing you are 21 or older. That is it. We check every single time, for every single person, no exceptions — including regulars we have known for years.

Golden hour runs 4pm to 6pm, every day, with rotating discounts on flower, pre-rolls, edibles and cartridge bundles. Pricing shows on the menu automatically during those hours — there is no code to remember and no minimum spend.

State limits for adult use are 28.5g of flower, 8g of concentrate, and 6 immature plants per day. Our menu tracks your basket against those limits as you shop, so you will know before you get to the counter rather than after.

Debit and cash at the counter, plus an ATM in the lobby if you need it. Credit cards are still off the table for cannabis retail nationwide, which is a banking rule rather than our preference.
