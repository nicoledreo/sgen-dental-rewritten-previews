Questions

The things people ask us.

Grouped the way you'd actually look for them.

Ordering

Do I need an account to order?

No. You can check out as a guest — we only need a name, phone number and a valid 21+ ID at the handoff.

Can I change an order after placing it?

Yes, until it's packed. Call (978) 555-0142 and we'll adjust it. Once it's out for delivery we can't modify the bag.

Delivery & pickup

What's the delivery cut-off?

9:30pm. Orders placed after that are queued for the next morning — call us if it's urgent and we'll tell you honestly whether we can make it.

Is there a delivery minimum?

$40 within Middleborough, $60 for the wider county. Delivery is free over $75.

Products & potency

Where are the lab results?

Every product page links its third-party COA by batch number. If a COA isn't posted, the product isn't on the menu.

I'm new — where should I start?

Shop by effect rather than strain, and start at 2.5mg for edibles. Our budtenders will talk you down from anything too strong.

Legal & ID

What ID do you accept?

A valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID showing you're 21 or over. We check it in store, at the curb and at the door.

Can I consume on site?

No. Massachusetts law prohibits consumption on licensed retail premises or in the parking area.
