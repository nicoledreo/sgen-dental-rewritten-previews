Before you order

The questions
we actually get.

A valid, unexpired government photo ID showing you are 21 or older — driver licence, state ID card, passport or military ID. We check it when you collect and again at the door on delivery. No ID, no handover, no exceptions, and we cannot refund an order you cannot collect.

We deliver anywhere within 18 miles of the Harbor St shop, Monday to Saturday. Orders placed before 9:30pm go out the same day; anything later lands first thing the next morning. Delivery is free over $75 and $7 below that.

Yes. Every figure on this page comes from the certificate of analysis for the specific batch currently on the shelf, not from a strain average. When a batch changes the number changes with it. Ask any budtender for the full COA and they will pull it up.

Start with the effect tabs rather than the strain names, pick Balanced, and buy the smallest quantity we sell. Take half of what you think is reasonable and wait a full forty minutes before deciding anything — especially with edibles, which are slow and easy to over-do.

Debit card and cash, in store and at the door. Federal banking rules mean we cannot take credit cards. There is an ATM in the lobby if you need it, and the price you see on the card is the price you pay — tax is already in it.
