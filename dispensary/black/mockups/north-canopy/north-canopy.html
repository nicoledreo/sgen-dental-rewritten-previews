The shop

One room, eleven farms, and a lot of rain.

North Canopy is a single room on Cedar Loop with pale oak shelves and too many ferns. We opened because the shops we liked kept turning into chains, and the ones that stayed small kept running out of the things we wanted.

We buy from eleven Washington farms. We know the growers by name, we know what the season did to each harvest, and we will tell you both without being asked. If something is not good this month, we will say so and point you at what is.
