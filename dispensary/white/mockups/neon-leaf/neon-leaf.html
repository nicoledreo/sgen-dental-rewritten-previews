About Neon Leaf

We built this for the shift that starts at midnight.

Las Vegas runs on people whose day begins when everyone else's ends — dealers, servers, sound engineers, nurses, rideshare drivers, and everyone who just got off a plane at 1am. For years the only thing open for them was a gas station.

So we stopped closing. Same full menu at 4am as at 4pm, same budtenders who actually know the shelf, same prices — better, after midnight. We're six minutes off the Strip with free parking out front, because paying for parking after a night out is its own insult.

No velvet rope, no lecture, no wellness sermon. Just a well-lit room that's open, someone behind the counter who's happy to see you, and a coffee machine by the door that is free and honestly not very good.

— Marisol Reyes, overnight lead · Neon Leaf, Las Vegas

Know before you go

The practical stuff, up front.

Especially if you're visiting. Nevada's rules are specific, and finding them out at the counter is nobody's idea of a good time.

ID & age

What ID do I need?

A valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID proving you're 21 or older — driver's licence, state ID, passport or passport card. Out-of-state and international IDs are fine. We check every visit, every time, including for delivery at the door.

Do I need a medical card?

No. Nevada allows adult-use (recreational) purchases for anyone 21+ with valid ID. Medical patients 18+ with a valid Nevada medical card get higher purchase limits and a tax break.

Can I bring someone under 21 inside?

No. Nobody under 21 can enter the sales floor, including infants, and we can't make exceptions — it's a condition of our licence.

Purchase limits

How much can I buy in one day?

Nevada adult-use limits are one ounce (28.35 g) of usable cannabis flower, or one-eighth of an ounce (3.5 g) of concentrate, per person per day. Edibles and other products count toward the limit by their THC equivalent. The limit resets daily and applies across all dispensaries, not just ours.

Can I take it home with me?

No. It's illegal to carry cannabis across state lines, including in checked baggage, and airports are federal jurisdiction. Buy what you'll use while you're here — the airport has amnesty boxes if you miscalculate.

Payment

What payment do you take?

Debit and cash. There's an ATM on site if you need it. We can't accept credit cards — cannabis remains federally illegal, so card networks won't process it. Delivery is prepaid by debit or paid by cash at the door.

Is there a fee on debit?

A small flat processing fee applies on debit, shown before you confirm. Cash has no fee and the on-site ATM has a standard withdrawal charge.

Where you can consume

Can I use it at my hotel?

Almost certainly not. Nearly every Las Vegas resort prohibits smoking cannabis on property, including in rooms and on casino floors, and can fine you. Check your specific property's policy before you assume.

Where is it actually legal to consume?

In a private residence with the owner's permission, or at a state-licensed cannabis consumption lounge. It is illegal to consume in public — that includes the Strip, sidewalks, parks, casinos, bars, and any vehicle, moving or parked, as driver or passenger. Public consumption carries a fine.

Can I drive after?

No. Nevada has per se impaired-driving limits for THC and enforces them. Plan on a rideshare — and note that transporting an opened container in the passenger cabin is also an offence.
